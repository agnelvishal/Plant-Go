# 🌿 PlantGo

**Map, protect and grow the world's medicinal and edible plants, flowers and fruits, and get rewarded for it.**

PlantGo is a mobile app with Web3 incentives that turns everyday plant spotting into a shared, living map of useful plants. Built at the **Edge City Buildathon**.

## What it does

- **Identify** any plant from a photo of its flower, leaf, fruit or bark, powered by the Pl@ntNet botanical database, with confidence scores, plant family and quick Wikipedia / Google links.
- **Map** sightings on a live community map. Filter by trees, flowers, medicinal or rare plants, search all sightings, and see a radar of what's growing nearby.
- **Protect** by vouching for other explorers' sightings so the map stays trustworthy, and reporting bad posts.
- **Grow** the community through a feed of discoveries, cheers, and profiles.
- **Earn** XP, levels and badges (Seedling → Explorer → Botanist → Tree Guardian) for identifying plants and reviewing sightings, the foundation for the Web3 reward layer that incentivises people to map, protect and grow useful plants.

## Tech

- Vanilla HTML / CSS / JavaScript, no framework or bundler
- Leaflet + OpenStreetMap for maps
- Capacitor for the Android app
- Firebase / Google sign-in, with a guest mode
- Node/Express + MariaDB REST API
